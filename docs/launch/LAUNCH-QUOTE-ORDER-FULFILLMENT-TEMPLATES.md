## Owner operations checklist
1. Receive branded inquiry. Restrict personal data to the authorized private inbox/tracker.
2. Generate internal lead ID. Confirm requirements, materials, build viability and safety review.
3. Calculate cost and delivery with no fabricated availability; obtain customer approval for engraving/design proof.
4. Send exact written quote with scope, taxes per verified dealer status, delivery, cancellation and validity.
5. Obtain customer acceptance; send verified payment link, never request card details by email/WhatsApp.
6. Verify payment in provider dashboard (not on screenshot supplied by customer); issue tax documents via approved accounting process.
7. Begin work only for approved safe products and after required proof/production gates.
8. Update private tracker: new lead → quote pending → quoted → accepted → payment verified → in production → shipped/picked up → completed; declines and refunds separate.
9. Confirm shipping cost, safe packing, customer notice and delivery record; respond to complaints or statutory rights requests.
10. Backups/retention/privacy requests follow verified policy. No customer PII in GitHub or public QA artifacts.
