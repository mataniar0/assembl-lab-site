## Interim operating controls / נהלי ביניים
1. Treat submissions as confidential. Limit access to owner and specifically authorized handlers; enable available account security controls and verify roles.
2. Review new inquiries regularly, distinguish brand/product, and avoid unnecessary copies; do not export to shared/public storage.
3. Review information **once every three months (owner-approved 2026-10-30)**. Assess whether each lead is active, converted to an order, needed for support/tax/legal duties or no longer necessary.
4. Do NOT invent an automatic retention period, delete invoices/required records, or claim vendor backups are erased. Remaining schedule details and statutory exceptions must be verified, distinguishing abandoned inquiries, completed orders, tax records, complaints and pending disputes.
5. To delete a record: verify record and scope, identify all copies (Formspree, mailbox, private tracker, exports), preserve legally required records, then execute authorized deletion and log outcome privately with no actual personal details in GitHub.
6. Avoid deletion on the basis of unverifiable requests. Verify requester identity proportionately without collecting excessive new personal data.
7. For access/correction requests: log privately (reference ID, received date, request type, verification, systems checked, decision and completion), review applicable rights/exceptions/deadlines with adviser, respond through verified contact address and document any limitations.
8. When deletion cannot be verified at vendor/backup level, explain the limit accurately, escalate to vendor and record follow-up instead of claiming complete removal.
9. On loss/unintended disclosure, restrict access, document incident and seek qualified advice on applicable notification/security obligations.

## Approved quarterly operational workflow (owner attestations, 2026-11-01)
1. Once every three months, review inquiry records available in Formspree and relevant Gmail notifications/threads. **Free plan's 30-day history may mean some earlier records are visible only in Gmail**; verify actual plan behavior before depending on dashboard completeness.
2. Separate open conversations, fulfilled orders, complaint/dispute records and legally required business documents from stale, closed/unneeded inquiries. Do not erase records with outstanding business/legal purpose.
3. Delete unnecessary inquiry records where available from Formspree dashboard and the corresponding Gmail messages/threads (including labels/archives), only after checking exceptions and dependencies. Take care not to delete correspondence needed for customer support or statutory accounting.
4. Record privately the review date, rough count by outcome and any vendor deletion limitation. Store no customer identifiers in public GitHub. Gmail Trash and Formspree backups must not be represented as immediately purged unless verified.
5. For privacy access/correction/deletion requests, verify requester proportionately, search both systems and account for provider-controlled copies; respond accurately on limits.
**Open:** Formspree provider retention/deletion/backup facts; Gmail Trash/retention semantics as actually configured; practical ability to remove all relevant mail and supplier copies; final approved rights-request operating timetable.
