# PST-230 — plant stand (digital design preview)

Source: mataniar0/assembl-lab-engineering PR #109, PST-230 V3, design-only.

This product page is a **CAD design preview, not a physically manufactured or approved product**. Top diameter 230 mm; total height 550 mm; 22 source CAD parts. The structural pieces are birch 6 mm and the flexible rim is birch 4 mm. Finger joints around the upper plate are retained, without interior top slots. Physical joint-fit, bending, load, stability, adhesive, and moisture durability remain unverified.
