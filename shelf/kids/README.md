# Kids Shelf — 700 × 220 mm

Final ready-to-cut kids shelf design.

## Main dimensions
- Overall width: 700 mm
- Shelf depth: 220 mm
- Material thickness: 6 mm
- Clear span between side brackets: 688 mm
- Side bracket rear height: 140 mm
- Side bracket front height: 55 mm
- Rear wall beam: 700 × 110 mm
- Front beam: 700 × 40 mm

## Design
- Smooth continuous lower curve on both side brackets
- Rocket + stars decorative cutouts
- Rear beam with 4 keyhole wall-mount slots
- Keyhole centers: 85 / 255 / 445 / 615 mm
- Existing side-bracket joint geometry preserved from the approved version
- No kerf compensation applied

## Production files
The ZIP in this folder contains:
1. `01_top_panel_700x220.dxf`
2. `02_side_bracket_left_rocket.dxf`
3. `03_side_bracket_right_rocket.dxf`
4. `04_rear_wall_beam_700x110_keyholes.dxf`
5. `05_front_beam_700x40.dxf`

The DXF geometry was checked against the final approved shelf geometry before upload.
