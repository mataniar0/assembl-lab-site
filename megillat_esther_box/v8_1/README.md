# Megillat Esther Box — V8.1

## Material
- Body and structural lid parts: 6 mm plywood
- Flexible curved lid skin: 4 mm plywood

## Main dimensions
- Internal clear space: 290 × 80 × 80 mm
- Body outside footprint: 302 × 92 mm
- Body wall height: 86 mm
- Lid rise: 28 mm

## Ring + pin hinge
Each side uses:
- PIN_DISC: Ø14.00 mm
- BODY_RING hole: Ø14.60 mm
- BODY_RING outside diameter: Ø28.00 mm
- LOCK_CAP: Ø22.00 mm
- Nominal radial pin clearance: 0.30 mm

## V8.1 reinforcement
- LID_END support-ear radius: 11 mm
- Target neck width: 12 mm
- Effective checked neck width: about 13 mm
- Rounded transition radius: about 5 mm

## Geometry checks
- Lid opening 0–110°: PASS
- Opening clearance through 120°: PASS
- Reinforced LID_END support vs END closed clearance: PASS
- Ring/pin nominal fit geometry: PASS
- Lock-cap retention: PASS
- Existing finger joints, bottom tabs and lid-beam joints: PASS

## Production note
No machine-specific kerf compensation is included. Test the ring/pin fit on the actual plywood before full production.
