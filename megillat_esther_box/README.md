# Megillat Esther Case — MEB-033 (current)

## Reported physical build: 15 November 2026
The maker reports that the 4 mm birch MEB-033 case was **cut and assembled successfully**, including the R6 bending. This is owner-reported prototype evidence; independent hinge, glue, load and dimensional acceptance tests have not been recorded.

The authoritative 2D design is in `mataniar0/assembl-lab-engineering` PR #113: 20 case parts + one inverse-curve glue jig on 440 × 800 mm stock. The R6 lid uses 22 rows of 4 slits, 88 total and 3 webs of nominal 4 mm in each row.

## Web 3D preview
- `media/meb033-assembly-preview.glb`: glTF 2.0 binary; 20 named meshes corresponding to the real product part profiles; nominal 4 mm thickness, units metres.
- Original 2D SVG part contours are extruded. Idealized curved roof contains the R6 cut pattern as openings of 0.42 mm display width; machine kerf is not calibrated.
- **Assembly locations are reconstructed approximately.** No owner photos or measured CAD assembly were available for fit comparison; some through-holes/details of side-hinge connections are not represented.
- The single glue jig is not part of the assembled model; the older product render remains a poster and must not be mistaken for the new 3D model.
- Do **not** derive CNC/laser profiles or dimensional tolerances from the GLB; use the authoritative MEB-033 DXF/SVG and verify the physical prototype.
- STEP / parametric CAD assembly has **not** been completed or independently verified.

Historical V8.1 / V8.2.2 production assets are retained as archived prior 6 mm-frame designs and should not be mistaken for current MEB-033 cuts.
